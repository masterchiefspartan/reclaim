## Mission Statement

> **"We are the mental side of recovery. Not physical."**
>
> Re:Claim helps people reclaim their life, identity, and emotional wellbeing
> during physical recovery. We're not tracking exercises or replacing PT -
> we're supporting the person behind the injury.

---

## Executive Summary

Re:Claim is a **mental wellness companion for people going through physical recovery**. While physical therapy addresses the body, Re:Claim addresses the mind - the frustration, fear, loss of identity, isolation, and emotional rollercoaster that comes with injury and recovery.

Through minimal onboarding, we understand the context of someone's recovery journey. Then through voice journaling and AI companionship, we help them:

- **Process** the emotional weight of their situation
- **Reclaim** their sense of self beyond the injury
- **Build** mental resilience for the recovery journey
- **Celebrate** progress that goes beyond physical milestones

## Part 1: Physical Therapy Recovery - Deep Research

### 1.1 The Recovery Journey Phases

| Phase                  | Timeline     | Physical State                              | Emotional State                              | Journaling Needs                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Acute**              | Days 1-14    | High pain, limited mobility, inflammation   | Shock, fear, frustration                     | Processing trauma, pain tracking              |
| **Subacute**           | Weeks 2-6    | Decreasing pain, beginning movement         | Impatience, hope, setbacks                   | Celebrating small wins, managing expectations |
| **Rehabilitation**     | Weeks 6-12+  | Building strength, increasing function      | Motivation fluctuations, plateau frustration | Progress tracking, goal visualization         |
| **Return to Activity** | Months 3-12+ | Near-normal function, rebuilding confidence | Fear of re-injury, excitement                | Building confidence, managing anxiety         |
| **Maintenance**        | Ongoing      | Preventing re-injury, optimal function      | Vigilance, acceptance                        | Long-term habits, prevention mindset          |

### 1.2 Key Information Categories for PT Recovery

#### A. Injury Profile

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. What part of your body was injured?
   → Body part selection with anatomy visualization
   → Supports multiple injuries

2. What type of injury is it?
   → Fracture, tear, strain, sprain, post-surgical, chronic condition
   → Determines vocabulary and expectations

3. When did this injury occur?
   → Date picker or "X weeks/months ago"
   → Calculates recovery timeline automatically

4. Did you have surgery?
   → Yes/No with follow-up: What procedure? When?
   → Surgical vs. conservative treatment paths differ significantly

5. What caused the injury?
   → Sports, accident, work, gradual onset, unknown
   → Helps AI understand context and relate to activities
```

#### B. Current Treatment Status

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. Are you currently in physical therapy?
   → Yes (how often?), No (why not?), Finished

7. What does your PT routine include?
   → Exercise types: stretching, strengthening, balance, cardio
   → Frequency and duration
   → Home exercise program?

8. Are you taking any medications for this injury?
   → Pain meds, anti-inflammatory, muscle relaxants
   → Helps understand pain management context

9. Do you use any assistive devices?
   → Crutches, brace, sling, wheelchair, cane
   → Indicates mobility level and daily challenges
```

#### C. Pain & Function Assessment

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. On a typical day, what's your pain level? (0-10)
    → Baseline for tracking improvement

11. What makes your pain worse?
    → Activities, positions, time of day, weather
    → AI can check in about these triggers

12. What makes your pain better?
    → Rest, ice, movement, medication, specific exercises
    → AI can suggest these during hard days

13. What activities are currently difficult for you?
    → Walking, stairs, sitting, sleeping, driving, work tasks
    → Personalized function tracking

14. What activities have you had to give up?
    → Sports, hobbies, work duties
    → Understanding the emotional weight of loss
```

#### D. Goals & Motivation

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
15. What's your primary recovery goal?
    → Return to sport, return to work, daily activities, pain-free living
    → Frames all progress conversations

16. What does "fully recovered" look like to you?
    → Open-ended, captures personal definition of success
    → May reveal unrealistic or achievable expectations

17. What's one activity you can't wait to do again?
    → Emotional anchor for motivation
    → AI can reference this during tough times

18. Have you recovered from injuries before?
    → Yes: What helped? What was hard?
    → Builds on past experience

19. What worries you most about this recovery?
    → Re-injury, permanent limitation, timeline, pain
    → AI can address specific fears
```

#### E. Lifestyle & Support Context

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
20. How does this injury affect your work?
    → Physical job, desk job, not working, modified duties
    → Understands daily demands

21. Who supports you in your recovery?
    → Family, friends, PT, doctor, online community, no one
    → Isolation vs. support network

22. How would you describe your sleep quality?
    → Pain affecting sleep is common and important
    → Sleep tracking integration opportunity

23. What time of day do you usually do your PT exercises?
    → Personalize reminder and check-in timing

24. How do you typically cope with frustration or setbacks?
    → Healthy coping, unhealthy coping, need strategies
    → Informs AI response style
```

#### F. Emotional Baseline

```
ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
25. How has this injury affected your mood overall?
    → Depression, anxiety, frustration, acceptance spectrum
    → Baseline for emotional tracking

26. On a scale of 1-10, how hopeful do you feel about recovery?
    → Hope is a key predictor of outcomes
    → Track over time

27. What's the hardest part of this recovery emotionally?
    → Open-ended, reveals core struggles
    → Loss of identity, dependence, uncertainty

28. Have you experienced anxiety or depression related to this injury?
    → May need additional resources
    → Sensitivity in AI responses
```

## Part 2: Agentic Onboarding Experience

### 2.1 Design Philosophy: LESS IS MORE

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         ONBOARDING PRINCIPLES                               │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                             │
│  ❌ OLD APPROACH (28 questions, 5-7 min)                                    │
│     → Too long, feels like a medical intake form                            │
│     → Users drop off or rush through                                        │
│     → Asks things we can learn from journaling                              │
│                                                                             │
│  ✅ NEW APPROACH (5 questions, 90 seconds)                                  │
│     → Get to value FAST                                                     │
│     → Ask only what's essential for Day 1 personalization                   │
│     → Learn everything else through journaling over time                    │
│     → Progressive profiling, not interrogation                              │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.3 What We DON'T Ask (And Why)

| Question             | Why We Skip It               | Our Philosophy                            |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Pain level (1-10)    | We're not a pain tracker     | Focus on emotions, not symptoms           |
| PT schedule          | We're not tracking exercises | We support the mind, PT supports the body |
| Medications          | Medical detail we don't need | We're not a medical app                   |
| Surgery details      | More than we need to know    | Simple context is enough                  |
| Physical limitations | PT territory                 | We care about emotional impact            |

**The shift:** Instead of "How's your knee?" we ask "How are YOU?"

### 2.3 What We DON'T Ask (And Why)

| Question          | Why Skip It            | How We Learn It Instead          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Pain triggers     | Too detailed for Day 1 | AI detects patterns from entries |
| PT schedule       | Not essential          | Ask when relevant in journal     |
| Medications       | Medical detail         | User mentions if relevant        |
| Emotional state   | Let them show us       | Mood tracking in journals        |
| Work impact       | Context detail         | Comes up naturally               |
| Support system    | Personal               | Emerges over time                |
| Coping strategies | Complex                | Learn from what works            |

## Part 3: Intelligent Journaling System

### 3.1 Context-Aware Prompting Engine

```typescript
interface JournalPromptContext {
  // Static context (from onboarding)
  profile: RecoveryProfile;

  // Dynamic context
  daysSinceInjury: number;
  daysSinceSurgery?: number;
  currentPhase: RecoveryPhase;
  recentEntries: JournalEntry[];
  recentMoodTrend: 'improving' | 'stable' | 'declining';
  recentPainTrend: 'improving' | 'stable' | 'worsening';

  // Temporal context
  timeOfDay: 'morning' | 'afternoon' | 'evening' | 'night';
  dayOfWeek: string;
  isPTDay: boolean;
  isExerciseTime: boolean;

  // Special context
  upcomingMilestone?: Milestone;
  recentSetback?: Setback;
  streakDays: number;
}

// Prompt generation based on context
function generatePrompt(context: JournalPromptContext): JournalPrompt {
  // Example logic:

  // Post-PT check-in
  if (context.isPTDay && context.timeOfDay === 'evening') {
    return {
      primary: `How did physical therapy go today? Any new exercises or progress to note?`,
      followUps: [
        `What did you work on with your PT?`,
        `How does your ${context.profile.injuryLocation} feel after the session?`,
        `Anything you want to remember for next time?`,
      ],
    };
  }

  // Pain spike response
  if (context.recentPainTrend === 'worsening') {
    return {
      primary: `I noticed your pain has been higher lately. What do you think might be contributing to that?`,
      followUps: [
        `Have you tried ${context.profile.painRelievers.join(' or ')}?`,
        `Is there anything different in your routine this week?`,
        `How are you feeling emotionally about the increased pain?`,
      ],
    };
  }

  // Milestone approaching
  if (context.upcomingMilestone) {
    return {
      primary: `You're ${context.upcomingMilestone.daysAway} days away from ${context.upcomingMilestone.name}. How are you feeling about it?`,
      followUps: [
        `What progress are you most proud of so far?`,
        `Any concerns as you approach this milestone?`,
      ],
    };
  }

  // Morning check-in
  if (context.timeOfDay === 'morning') {
    return {
      primary: `Good morning! How did you sleep, and how's your ${context.profile.injuryLocation} feeling this morning?`,
      followUps: [`What's on your recovery agenda today?`, `Any exercises planned?`],
    };
  }

  // ... more contextual prompts
}
```

interface RecoveryProfile {

    ptClinic?: string;

    exerciseTypes: ExerciseType[];
    medications: Medication[];

  };

  baseline: {
    painLevel: number;
    painTriggers: string[];
    painRelievers: string[];
    difficultActivities: string[];
    lostActivities: string[];
    functionLevel: number;
  };

  // Goals
  goals: {
    primary: string;
    definitionOfRecovered: string;
    activityToReturn: string;
    shortTermGoals: Goal[];
    longTermGoals: Goal[];
  };
